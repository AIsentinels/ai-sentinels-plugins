# People & Ops (`ai-sentinels-people-ops`)

People and operations: HR policy answers, onboarding, interviews and reviews; process docs, runbooks, status reports, risk and vendor reviews; stakeholder updates and planning.

Part of the **AI Sentinels** workshop catalogue by AI Sentinels. Install only the packs your role needs; run `ai-sentinels-setup` from the Core pack for a recommendation.
